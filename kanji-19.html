How to remember it

Rain 雨 above, and below it the tail of the lightning 电 twisting down out of the cloud. Lightning inside the rain is electricity.

Stroke order 13 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 電車でんしゃtrain
    電車で会社に行きます。I go to work by train.
  2. 電話でんわtelephone; phone call
    役所に電話しました。I called the government office.
  3. 電気でんきelectricity; light
    部屋の電気を消してください。Please turn off the light in the room.
  4. 停電ていでんpower outage
    台風で停電しました。The typhoon caused a power outage.
  5. 電池でんちbattery
    時計の電池を替えます。I will change the watch battery.
