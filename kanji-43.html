#421 most common3rd grade7 strokesradical 豆parts 口
ON
トウズ
KUN
まめまめ

How to remember it

Originally a footed serving dish: a lid across the top, the bowl drawn as a mouth 口, and the stand underneath. What was served in it — beans — took over the character.

Stroke order 7 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 豆まめbean
    節分に豆をまきます。We throw beans at Setsubun.
  2. 大豆だいずsoybean
    みそは大豆から作ります。Miso is made from soybeans.
  3. 納豆なっとうnatto
    朝は納豆を食べます。I eat natto in the morning.
  4. 豆腐とうふtofu
    豆腐を一丁ください。One block of tofu, please.
  5. 枝豆えだまめedamame
    枝豆とビールを頼みました。I ordered edamame and a beer.

How to remember it

The sun 日 sitting right on top of 者 'person' — the 耂 top over earth 土. The sun beating down on someone out in the open: summer heat.

Stroke order 12 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 暑いあついhot (weather)
    今日はとても暑いです。It is very hot today.
  2. 暑さあつさthe heat
    暑さで眠れませんでした。I could not sleep because of the heat.
  3. 蒸し暑いむしあついhumid and hot
    東京の夏は蒸し暑いです。Summer in Tokyo is hot and humid.
  4. 残暑ざんしょlingering summer heat
    今年は残暑が続きます。The late-summer heat is lasting this year.
  5. 猛暑もうしょheat wave
    猛暑で体調を崩しました。The heat wave made me ill.
