#381 most common2nd grade12 strokesradical 日parts 日 青 龶 月

セイ
KUN
はれるはれはれ--ばれはらす

How to remember it

The sun 日 on the left and 青, blue — the plant sign 龶 over 月 — on the right. Sun plus blue sky is exactly what it means for the weather to clear.

Stroke order 12 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 晴れるはれるto clear up
    午後には晴れるでしょう。It should clear up in the afternoon.
  2. 晴れはれfine weather
    明日の天気は晴れです。Tomorrow's weather is fine.
  3. 晴天せいてんfine weather
    晴天の日に洗濯する。I do laundry on a fine day.
  4. 快晴かいせいclear skies
    今日は快晴で気持ちいい。It is beautifully clear today.
  5. 晴れ間はれまbreak in the weather
    雨の晴れ間に外へ出た。I went out during a break in the rain.
