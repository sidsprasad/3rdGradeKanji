How to remember it

A flat body of roof (尸) laid over 至, arrive. The place you come home to and lie down under, and by extension the shop that sells one thing.

Stroke order 9 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 部屋へやroom
    部屋を掃除しました。I cleaned my room.
  2. 屋根やねroof
    屋根から雪が落ちます。Snow falls off the roof.
  3. 本屋ほんやbookstore
    駅の近くに本屋があります。There is a bookstore near the station.
  4. 八百屋やおやgreengrocer
    八百屋で野菜を買います。I buy vegetables at the greengrocer.
  5. 屋上おくじょうrooftop
    屋上から街が見えます。You can see the town from the rooftop.

How to remember it

    読書が趣味です。Reading is my hobby.
