department / course

#281 most common2nd grade9 strokesradical 禾parts 禾 丿 木 斗 丶
ON
カ
KUN
—

How to remember it

Grain (禾) being measured out with a dipper (斗). You sort the harvest into measured lots, and sorting things into lots is what a department or a course of study does.

Stroke order 9 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 科学かがくscience
    科学の本を読みます。I read science books.
  2. 教科書きょうかしょtextbook
    教科書を持ってきてください。Please bring your textbook.
  3. 科目かもくsubject; course
    好きな科目は何ですか。What is your favorite subject?
  4. 内科ないかinternal medicine
    内科で診てもらいました。I was seen in internal medicine.
  5. 外科げかsurgery (department)
    外科の受付はあちらです。The surgery reception is over there.

thin / fine

#282 most common2nd grade11 strokesradical 糸parts 糸 田
ON
サイ
KUN
ほそいほそるこまかこまかい

How to remember it

Thread (糸) beside a rice field (田). Seen from far away the field is a grid of hair-thin lines, so 細 means thin, fine, minutely detailed.

Stroke order 11 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 細かいこまかいfine; detailed; small
    細かいお金がありません。I do not have any small change.
  2. 細いほそいthin; narrow
    この道は細いです。This road is narrow.
  3. 詳細しょうさいdetails
    詳細はメールで送ります。I will send the details by email.
  4. 明細めいさいitemized statement
    給料の明細を確認します。I check my pay statement.
  5. 細部さいぶthe fine details
    細部まで丁寧に作ります。It is made carefully down to the fine details.

How to remember it

The nine (九) shape with a dot tucked into its curve: a person bent over into a ball. Three strokes that curl in on themselves, so the meaning is round.

Stroke order 3 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 丸まるcircle; a round mark
    正しい答えに丸をつけます。Put a circle by the correct answer.
  2. 丸いまるいround
    丸いテーブルを買いました。I bought a round table.
  3. 丸めるまるめるto roll up; to crumple
    紙を丸めて捨てました。I crumpled the paper up and threw it away.
  4. 日の丸ひのまるthe Japanese flag
    学校に日の丸が上がっています。The Japanese flag is flying at the school.
  5. 丸ごとまるごとwhole; in one piece
    りんごを丸ごと食べました。I ate the apple whole.
