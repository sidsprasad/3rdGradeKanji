How to remember it

The king (王) with one extra dot - the jade bead he wears. Originally three discs of jade threaded on a string.

Stroke order 5 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 玉たまball; bead; jewel
    子どもが玉を投げます。The child throws the ball.
  2. 玉子たまごegg
    玉子を二つ使います。I use two eggs.
  3. 目玉めだまeyeball; main attraction
    これが今日の目玉商品です。This is today's featured bargain.
  4. 玉ねぎたまねぎonion
    玉ねぎを細かく切ります。Chop the onion finely.
  5. 百円玉ひゃくえんだまhundred-yen coin
    百円玉が二枚あります。I have two hundred-yen coins.

Earth (土) piled on top of 干. The old shape was a pair of handcuffs: to be spared them was the greatest luck, so the sign came to mean good fortune.
