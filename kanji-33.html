321

autumn

#321 most common2nd grade9 strokesradical 禾parts 禾 丿 木 火
ON
シュウ
KUN
あきとき

How to remember it

Grain (禾) standing next to fire (火). Autumn is when the grain is cut and the leftover stubble is burned off in the field.

Stroke order 9 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 秋あきautumn
    秋は野菜が安いです。Vegetables are cheap in autumn.
  2. 秋風あきかぜautumn breeze
    秋風が気持ちいいです。The autumn breeze feels nice.
  3. 秋晴れあきばれclear autumn weather
    今日は秋晴れです。Today is clear autumn weather.
  4. 秋分しゅうぶんautumn equinox
    秋分の日は祝日です。The autumn equinox is a public holiday.
  5. 立秋りっしゅうfirst day of autumn
    もう立秋を過ぎました。The first day of autumn has already passed.
