#161 most common3rd grade9 strokesradical 口parts 口

KUN

How to remember it

Three mouths 口 stacked in a pyramid, one on top and two below. A crowd of mouths tasting and grading the goods; what they all approve is a quality article.

Stroke order 9 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 商品しょうひんmerchandise; product
    この商品は人気です。This product is popular.
  2. 品物しなものgoods; article
    品物を店に返しました。I returned the goods to the store.
  3. 食品しょくひんfood products
    食品売り場は地下です。The food section is in the basement.
  4. 作品さくひんa work (of art)
    母の作品が選ばれました。My mother's work was selected.
  5. 部品ぶひんparts; components
    車の部品が届きました。The car parts arrived.

On the left 歹, bare bones under a stroke; on the right 匕, a person bent over kneeling. A living person bowing beside a skeleton is death.
