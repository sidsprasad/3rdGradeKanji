201

role / duty

#201 most common3rd grade7 strokesradical 彳parts 彳 亻 殳 几 丿 又
ON
ヤクエキ
KUN
—

How to remember it

The 彳 step radical on the left is a man setting out on the road, and 殳 on the right is a hand 又 gripping a weapon. A man marching off on official service — a duty, a post, a role.

Stroke order 7 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 役所やくしょgovernment office
    役所で書類をもらう。I pick up the forms at the government office.
  2. 市役所しやくしょcity hall
    市役所で手続きをした。I did the paperwork at city hall.
  3. 役員やくいんdirector; officer
    父は会社の役員です。My father is a company director.
  4. 主役しゅやくleading role
    今日の主役は彼女です。She is the star today.
  5. 現役げんえきactive duty; still working
    祖父はまだ現役です。My grandfather is still working.
