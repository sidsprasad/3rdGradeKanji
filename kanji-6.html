KUN
いる-いる-いりいれる-いれはいる

How to remember it

An arrow-head pointing down into an opening — going IN. (Compare 人: 入 has the left stroke on top.)

Stroke order 2 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 入るはいるto enter
    部屋に入ります。I enter the room.
  2. 入れるいれるto put in
    かばんに本を入れます。I put the book in my bag.
  3. 入口いりぐちentrance
    入口はこちらです。The entrance is this way.
  4. 入学にゅうがくentering a school
    四月に入学します。I enter school in April.
  5. 記入きにゅうfilling in (a form)
    名前を記入してください。Please fill in your name.
