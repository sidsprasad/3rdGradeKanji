401

bow / thanks

#401 most common3rd grade5 strokesradical 礻parts 礻 乙
ON
レイライ
KUN
—

How to remember it

The altar radical 礻 on the left, and on the right 乙, a figure bent over at the waist. Bowing before the altar is what courtesy and thanks look like.

Stroke order 5 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 礼れいthanks; a bow
    先生に礼を言いました。I thanked the teacher.
  2. お礼おれいthanks; a thank-you gift
    お礼の手紙を書きます。I will write a thank-you letter.
  3. 失礼しつれいrudeness; excuse me
    お先に失礼します。Excuse me for leaving first.
  4. 礼儀れいぎmanners; etiquette
    礼儀を大切にしています。I take good manners seriously.
  5. 礼金れいきんkey money (to a landlord)
    礼金はいくらですか。How much is the key money?

How to remember it

A sun 日 at the bottom with strokes piled on top of it — 廾 crossed by 十. Day stacked on day on day until they add up to the distant past.
