How to remember it

The grass crown 艹 over a person 人 standing under a tree 木. Leaves picked from the bush and carried home to be brewed: tea.

Stroke order 9 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. お茶おちゃtea
    お茶を入れましょうか。Shall I make some tea?
  2. 茶色ちゃいろbrown
    茶色のかばんを買った。I bought a brown bag.
  3. 紅茶こうちゃblack tea
    朝は紅茶を飲みます。I drink black tea in the morning.
  4. 喫茶店きっさてんcoffee shop
    駅前の喫茶店で待つ。I will wait at the coffee shop by the station.
  5. 茶道さどうtea ceremony
    妻は茶道を習っている。My wife is learning the tea ceremony.

Wood 木 beside 主, master — a lamp flame 丶 above the stand 王. The piece of timber that is master of the house is the pillar holding it up.

How to remember it

A piece of meat 月 and a hand held above the altar 示. Meat set on the altar by hand is an offering, and the offering is the festival.

Stroke order 11 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 祭りまつりfestival
    町の祭りに参加した。I took part in the town festival.
  2. 祭るまつるto enshrine
    神社に神を祭る。A deity is enshrined at the shrine.
  3. 夏祭りなつまつりsummer festival
    夏祭りで花火を見た。I watched fireworks at the summer festival.
  4. 祭日さいじつnational holiday
    祭日は役所が閉まる。Government offices close on national holidays.
  5. 文化祭ぶんかさいschool festival
    来週は学校の文化祭だ。The school festival is next week.
