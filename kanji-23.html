How to remember it

穴 a cave on top — a roof 宀 with 八 the opening beneath it — and 九 nine underneath. You go down the hole all nine ways, right to the very bottom of the matter.

Stroke order 7 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 研究けんきゅうresearch
    大学で研究を続けます。I am continuing my research at university.
  2. 研究所けんきゅうじょresearch institute
    母は研究所に勤めている。My mother works at a research institute.
  3. 究極きゅうきょくultimate
    究極の目標は合格です。The ultimate goal is passing.
  4. 追究ついきゅうinvestigation; pursuit
    事故の原因を追究する。They are looking into the cause of the accident.
  5. 究めるきわめるto master; to get to the bottom of
    日本語を究めたいです。I want to master Japanese.
