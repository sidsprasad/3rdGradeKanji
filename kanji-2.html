#11 most common1st grade4 strokesradical 丨parts 口 丨
ON
チュウ
KUN
なかうちあたる

How to remember it

A line driven straight through the centre of a box — dead centre.

Stroke order 4 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 中なかinside; middle
    かばんの中に本があります。There is a book inside the bag.
  2. 中学校ちゅうがっこうjunior high school
    娘は中学校に通っています。My daughter goes to junior high school.
  3. 一日中いちにちじゅうall day long
    一日中働きました。I worked all day long.
  4. 中心ちゅうしんcentre; heart of
    町の中心に住んでいます。I live in the centre of town.
  5. 中国ちゅうごくChina
    中国は大きな国です。China is a big country.

ON
サンゾウ
KUN
みみつみっつ

How to remember it

Three fingers laid flat, one above the other. Note the proportions when you write it: the middle stroke is the shortest and the bottom stroke the longest, so the character sits steady rather than toppling.

Stroke order 3 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 三つみっつthree (things)
    みかんを三つ食べました。I ate three mandarins.
  2. 三人さんにんthree people
    家族は三人です。There are three people in my family.
  3. 三月さんがつMarch
    三月に卒業します。I graduate in March.
  4. 三日みっかthe 3rd; three days
    三日かかりました。It took three days.
  5. 三時さんじthree o'clock
    三時にお茶を飲みます。I drink tea at three.

Write it

The altar radical (礻) beside earth (土) — a mound of sacred earth, a shrine. Companies grew out of these local associations.

Words to know

  1. 会社かいしゃcompany
    会社まで歩きます。I walk to the office.
  2. 社会しゃかいsociety
    社会に出て働きます。I go out into society and work.
  3. 神社じんじゃShinto shrine
    神社にお参りします。I visit the shrine to pray.
  4. 社長しゃちょうcompany president
    社長は今、出かけています。The president is out right now.
  5. 社員しゃいんcompany employee
    父は会社の社員です。My father is a company employee.
