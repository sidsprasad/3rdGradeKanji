411

cry / ring

#411 most common2nd grade14 strokesparts 口 鳥 灬
ON
メイ
KUN
なくなるならす

How to remember it

A mouth 口 beside a bird 鳥, whose tail feathers are the four dots 灬 underneath. A bird opening its mouth: it sings, and anything else that sounds off.

Stroke order 14 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 鳴るなるto ring; to sound
    電話が鳴っています。The phone is ringing.
  2. 鳴くなくto chirp; to cry (animal)
    庭で鳥が鳴いている。A bird is chirping in the garden.
  3. 鳴らすならすto ring (something)
    ベルを鳴らしてください。Please ring the bell.
  4. 悲鳴ひめいscream
    外で悲鳴が聞こえた。I heard a scream outside.
  5. 耳鳴りみみなりringing in the ears
    最近耳鳴りがします。My ears have been ringing lately.

How to remember it

Almost 大, but with a short stroke laid across the top: that is the notch and the feathers, and the long stroke down the middle is the shaft.

Words to know

  1. 矢やarrow
    矢が的に当たりました。The arrow hit the target.
  2. 矢印やじるしarrow (symbol)
    矢印の方へ進んでください。Please go in the direction of the arrow.
  3. 弓矢ゆみやbow and arrow
    弓矢は昔の武器です。The bow and arrow is an old weapon.
  4. 矢先やさきthe very moment; arrowhead
    出かけた矢先に雨が降った。It rained the moment I went out.
  5. 矢継ぎ早やつぎばやin rapid succession
    矢継ぎ早に質問されました。I was asked questions in rapid succession.
