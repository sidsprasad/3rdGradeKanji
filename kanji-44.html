#431 most common2nd grade3 strokesparts 扌 亅
ON
サイ
KUN
—

How to remember it

Almost the hand radical 扌, but the hook 亅 cuts straight through the crossbar: a sprout pushing up out of the ground. Ability that was there from the start.

Stroke order 3 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 才能さいのうtalent
    彼女は絵の才能があります。She has a talent for drawing.
  2. 天才てんさいgenius
    あの子は天才ですね。That child is a genius.
  3. 才さいyears old
    息子は五才になりました。My son has turned five.
  4. 秀才しゅうさいbright student
    兄はクラスの秀才でした。My brother was the star student of his class.
  5. 多才たさいversatile; many-talented
    多才な人だと思います。I think he is a many-talented person.

How to remember it

龶 on top — the ear of grain, three strokes crossed by a vertical — and a foot 夂 stepping below. Winter wheat is the crop you walk out and tread down.

How to remember it

自 on top was itself the original drawing of a nose. Once 自 was taken over to mean 'self', a base was built under it: a field 田 on a stand 廾.

#434 most common1st grade7 strokesradical 貝parts 目 八
ON
バイ
KUN
かい

How to remember it

An eye 目 standing on two legs 八 — really a cowrie shell, its slit down the middle and its feelers trailing below. Shells were money, which is why 貝 hides inside 買 and 貨.

Stroke order 7 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 貝かいshellfish; shell
    海で貝を拾いました。I picked up shells at the beach.
  2. 貝殻かいがらseashell
    貝殻を集めています。I collect seashells.
  3. 巻貝まきがいspiral shell; snail
    岩に巻貝がついています。There are spiral shells on the rock.
  4. 二枚貝にまいがいbivalve
    あさりは二枚貝です。Clams are bivalves.
  5. 貝類かいるいshellfish (as a category)
    貝類のアレルギーがあります。I have a shellfish allergy.

How to remember it

Two strokes only: the curved back of the blade, and the handle hooking down from it. Squeeze the same shape thin and it becomes the 刂 on the right of 切 and 別.
