above / up

#31 most common1st grade3 strokesparts 卜 一
ON
ジョウショウシャン
KUN
うえうえうわかみあげる-あげるあがる-あがるあがり-あがりのぼるのぼりのぼせるのぼすたてまつる

How to remember it

A ground line with a mark standing UP above it. Write the tall vertical first, then the short right-hand tick, then the base line last — and remember it as the exact mirror of 下, which hangs its mark below the line instead.

Stroke order 3 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 上うえabove; on top
    机の上に本があります。There is a book on the desk.
  2. 上がるあがるto go up; to rise
    値段が上がりました。The price went up.
  3. 上手じょうずskilful; good at
    日本語が上手ですね。Your Japanese is good, isn't it?
  4. 以上いじょうmore than; above
    十年以上住んでいます。I have lived here more than ten years.
  5. 上げるあげるto raise; to give
    手を上げてください。Please raise your hand.

Write it
