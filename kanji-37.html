361

clothes / obey

#361 most common3rd grade8 strokesradical 月parts 月 卩 又
ON
フク
KUN
—

How to remember it

The 月 on the left is the flesh-and-body radical; on the right a kneeling person 卩 has a hand 又 laid over them, pulling the garment on. Clothing goes onto a body, and the one who kneels obeys.

Stroke order 8 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 服ふくclothes
    新しい服を買いました。I bought new clothes.
  2. 洋服ようふくWestern clothes
    洋服をたんすにしまう。I put my clothes away in the dresser.
  3. 制服せいふくuniform
    娘は制服で学校へ行く。My daughter goes to school in her uniform.
  4. 服装ふくそうattire; dress
    面接の服装に気をつける。I am careful about what I wear to the interview.
  5. 和服わふくJapanese clothes
    祖母は和服が似合う。Japanese clothes suit my grandmother.

How to remember it

Ground 土 on top and the measuring hand 寸 below: land carefully measured off and set aside. The plot that was marked out became the temple.

Stroke order 6 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 寺てらtemple
    山の上に古い寺がある。There is an old temple on the mountain.
  2. お寺おてらtemple (polite)
    正月にお寺へ行きます。We go to the temple at New Year.
  3. 寺院じいんBuddhist temple
    京都には寺院が多い。There are many temples in Kyoto.
  4. 寺社じしゃtemples and shrines
    町の寺社を見て回った。I went around looking at the town's temples and shrines.
  5. 寺子屋てらこやEdo-period school
    昔は寺子屋で字を習った。Long ago people learned writing at temple schools.

How to remember it

自 was originally a drawing of a nose, and it sits right on top of the heart 心. Air from the nose down to the heart is breath.

Words to know

  1. 息いきbreath
    大きく息をすってください。Please take a deep breath.
  2. 息子むすこson
    息子は今年で十歳です。My son turns ten this year.
  3. ため息ためいきsigh
    父はため息をついた。My father let out a sigh.
  4. 休息きゅうそくrest
    少し休息を取りましょう。Let's take a short rest.
  5. 利息りそくinterest (on money)
    銀行の利息は低いです。Bank interest rates are low.
