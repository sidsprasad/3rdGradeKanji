How to remember it

An arrow 矢 flying out of a mouth 口 — words that go straight to the mark, because the one speaking already knows.

Words to know

  1. 知るしるto know
    その言葉を知っています。I know that word.
  2. 知らせるしらせるto inform; to let know
    結果を知らせてください。Please let me know the result.
  3. 知識ちしきknowledge
    法律の知識が必要です。You need some knowledge of the law.
  4. 通知つうちnotice; notification
    市から通知が届きました。A notice arrived from the city.
  5. 知事ちじprefectural governor
    来月、県知事の選挙があります。There is an election for prefectural governor next month.
