Words to know

  1. 方かたperson (polite); way of doing
    あの方は先生です。That person is a teacher.

    いい方法を考えます。I will think of a good method.
  3. 夕方ゆうがたevening
    夕方に帰ります。I will come home in the evening.

    方向が分かりません。I do not know the direction.

    一方の意見も聞きます。I will listen to the other side's opinion too.
