How to remember it

A person 亻 on the left beside 可, which is a 口 mouth with a bent stroke coming out of it. A person opening their mouth to put a question: what?

Stroke order 7 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 何なにwhat
    何を飲みますか。What would you like to drink?
  2. 何時なんじwhat time
    会議は何時からですか。What time does the meeting start?
  3. 何人なんにんhow many people
    家族は何人ですか。How many people are in your family?
  4. 何度なんどhow many times
    何度も練習しました。I practiced it many times.
  5. 何かなにかsomething; anything
    何か質問はありますか。Do you have any questions?

A bell hanging in its frame 冂, with 十 above as the hook and 干 inside as the clapper — a bronze bell of the southern people. Their direction became the word for south.
