How to remember it

A big eye (目) up on a pair of legs (儿) — an eye walking around, looking at everything.

Stroke order 7 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 見るみるto see; to watch
    テレビを見ます。I watch television.
  2. 見せるみせるto show
    写真を見せてください。Please show me the photo.
  3. 意見いけんopinion
    意見を言ってください。Please give your opinion.
  4. 見学けんがくstudy visit; tour
    工場を見学しました。We toured the factory.
  5. 花見はなみcherry-blossom viewing
    春に花見をします。We go blossom viewing in spring.
