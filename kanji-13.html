How to remember it

The hand radical 扌 beside 旨, a spoon 匕 over the sun 日 — the hand reaches out and its finger points at what it wants a taste of.

Words to know

    地図で駅を指します。I point to the station on the map.

How to remember it

A picture of the organ itself: the curved bowl of the muscle with three drops of blood flicking off it. Write the hook first, then the three strokes around it.

    町の中心に住んでいます。I live in the center of town.

    政治に関心があります。I am interested in politics.

How to remember it

Bamboo 竹 laid over 弔, a bow 弓 bound with cord — bamboo grows one joint after another, and the cord is wound round in order. 第 marks a thing's number in the sequence.

One 一 stroke laid on the head of 白, white — add a single mark to the white and the count jumps to a hundred. Write 一, then 白 underneath.
