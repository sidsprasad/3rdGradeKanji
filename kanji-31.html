flower

#301 most common1st grade7 strokesradical 艹parts 艹 化 亻 匕
ON
カケ
KUN
はな

How to remember it

Grass (艹) over 化, change, which is a standing person (亻) beside a bent one (匕). The plant transforms, and the change it makes is a flower.

Stroke order 7 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 花はなflower
    庭に花が咲きました。Flowers bloomed in the garden.
  2. 花見はなみcherry-blossom viewing
    公園で花見をしました。We went blossom viewing in the park.
  3. 花火はなびfireworks
    花火大会に行きます。I am going to the fireworks festival.
  4. 花屋はなやflorist
    駅前の花屋で買いました。I bought it at the florist by the station.
  5. 花束はなたばbouquet
    母に花束を渡しました。I handed my mother a bouquet.

spring (season)

#302 most common2nd grade9 strokesradical 日parts 𡗗 三 一 大 人 日
ON
シュン
KUN
はる

How to remember it

Three shoots crowding up (𡗗, a 三 crossed by a big 大) with the sun (日) underneath. Plants pushing up over the warming sun is springtime.

Stroke order 9 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 春はるspring
    春は暖かいです。Spring is warm.
  2. 春休みはるやすみspring vacation
    春休みに旅行します。I will travel over the spring vacation.
  3. 青春せいしゅんyouth
    青春の思い出です。It is a memory from my youth.
  4. 春分しゅんぶんthe vernal equinox
    春分の日は祝日です。Vernal Equinox Day is a public holiday.
  5. 立春りっしゅんfirst day of spring
    立春を過ぎても寒いです。It is still cold even after the first day of spring.

How to remember it

Originally a big person (大) standing over a fire; the brush flattened the top into 土 and splayed the flames below. A body lit red by the blaze.

Words to know

  1. 赤あかred
    信号が赤になりました。The light turned red.
  2. 赤いあかいred (adjective)
    赤いかばんを買いました。I bought a red bag.

    赤ちゃんが生まれました。A baby was born.

How to remember it

A young sprout (龶) growing above 月. Fresh growth is the blue-green color, which is why 青 covers both blue and the green of a traffic light.
