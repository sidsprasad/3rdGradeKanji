291

shore / bank

#291 most common3rd grade8 strokesradical 山parts 山 厂 干 十
ON
ガン
KUN
きし

How to remember it

A mountain (山) above a cliff (厂) with 干 beneath. Where the high rock breaks off and the water stops is the shore.

Stroke order 8 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 岸きしshore; bank
    船が岸に着きました。The boat reached the shore.
  2. 海岸かいがんcoast; seashore
    海岸を散歩しました。I walked along the coast.
  3. 川岸かわぎしriverbank
    川岸に桜が咲いています。Cherry blossoms are blooming on the riverbank.
  4. 対岸たいがんthe opposite bank
    対岸にビルが見えます。You can see buildings on the opposite bank.
  5. 彼岸ひがんthe equinox week
    彼岸に墓参りをします。We visit the family grave during the equinox week.

ON
キャクカク
KUN

How to remember it

Under a roof (宀) sits 各, which is a foot (夂) coming down toward a mouth (口). Feet arrive at your door and you feed them: a guest.

Stroke order 9 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 客きゃくguest; customer
    今日は客が多いです。There are a lot of customers today.
  2. お客さんおきゃくさんcustomer; guest (polite)
    お客さんが来ました。A customer has arrived.
  3. 乗客じょうきゃくpassenger
    乗客が電車を降ります。The passengers get off the train.
  4. 観光客かんこうきゃくtourist
    観光客で駅が混んでいます。The station is crowded with tourists.
  5. 客室きゃくしつguest room
    ホテルの客室は静かです。The hotel guest rooms are quiet.

How to remember it

A sail-like frame (几) with an insect (虫) shut inside. The old belief was that wind was born from creatures stirring, so the bug sits in the middle of the gust.

Stroke order 9 strokes — grey shows what you have already drawn

Words to know

  1. 風かぜwind
    今日は風が強いです。The wind is strong today.
  2. 台風たいふうtyphoon
    台風が近づいています。A typhoon is approaching.
  3. 風邪かぜa cold (illness)
    風邪をひいて休みました。I caught a cold and took the day off.
  4. 風呂ふろbath
    毎晩風呂に入ります。I take a bath every night.
  5. 和風わふうJapanese style
    和風の家に住んでいます。I live in a Japanese-style house.
